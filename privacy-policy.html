ReaDex — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-28 · Last updated 2026-10-28

What ReaDex is

ReaDex is a personal reader client. It is conceptually equivalent to a web browser or an IPTV client: the user supplies the URL of a content source, and the app renders what that source returns. ReaDex ships with no built-in catalog, no bundled content, and no curated list of third-party sites. The author does not host, scrape, or redistribute content on behalf of any user. Responsibility for the legality of any configured content source lies with the end user.

What data ReaDex collects about you

None. ReaDex contains no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, and no advertising SDKs. The app owner does not receive any information about who you are, which device you use, which source you configured, what you read, or when you used the app.

What data stays on your device

The following items are stored locally in the app's own sandbox on your phone, and never leave it:

  • Your configured content source URL and the optional secondary URLs (covers CDN, curated list)
  • Your reading history, library, favourites, and tag affinity signals used to build the local "Recommended for you" row
  • Downloaded chapters, cached pages, and cover thumbnails
  • App preferences (language, content rating filters, image quality)

Any of this can be wiped from the device at any time via Settings → Delete everything.

What data your configured source sees

When you tell ReaDex which content source URL to use, the app makes HTTPS requests to that URL on your behalf, the same way a web browser would. The operator of whichever source you configured can see those requests (IP address, HTTP headers, which items you asked for) under their own privacy policy, which is not controlled by ReaDex or its author. The app rejects non-HTTPS URLs at setup.

Permissions ReaDex uses

  • Internet access — to reach the content source you configured.
  • File storage inside the app's own sandbox — to cache downloads and database state.

ReaDex does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos, calendar, call log, SMS, or any system-wide storage area.

Children

ReaDex is not directed at children under 13. Because the content shown is determined by whichever source the user configures, parents and guardians are responsible for deciding whether the configured source is age-appropriate for their household.
